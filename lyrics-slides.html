Slide breaks: each blank line starts a new slide. Lines between blank lines stay on one slide — the font auto-shrinks to fit.

Sections: mark headings with a leading slash like /V1 or /Chorus. Lines under the heading define that section. Reuse it later by writing the same heading on its own line (e.g. /Chorus) — the previous content plays again. Add x2 / x3 for multiple repeats (e.g. /Chorus x2). Heading names match case-insensitively.

Blank slides: use /blank (or /black, /silence) on its own line to drop a blank slide in. Repeats with x2 too.
